QUICK ANSWERS
═════════════

Q: What are we training?
A: MLP neural network (16.7M parameters) that predicts adaptive
   apodization weights for ultrasound beamforming.
   See: README_TRAINING_EXPLANATION.md (Section 1)

Q: How does it help?
A: Replaces uniform DAS weights with learned adaptive weights.
   Results: 93.7% error reduction in 100 steps.
   See: README_TRAINING_EXPLANATION.md (Section: How It Improves)

Q: Is the objective function implemented?
A: YES. Fully implemented in able_plus_plus/networks/losses.py
   (lines 16-49) and called every training step.
   See: OBJECTIVE_FUNCTION_DETAILED.md (Section: Is It Implemented)

Q: Where is the objective function?
A: Defined:
   - smsle_loss()         (line 16)
   - unity_gain_penalty() (line 30)
   - total_loss()         (line 40)
   All in: able_plus_plus/networks/losses.py
   
   Called:
   - able_plus_plus/train.py (line 37)
   - run_training.py (line 123)
   See: OBJECTIVE_FUNCTION_DETAILED.md (Section: Code Locations)

Q: What's the objective function?
A: L_total = 0.8 * L_SMSLE + 0.2 * L_unity
   
   80% term: reconstruction accuracy (log-domain error)
   20% term: weight plausibility (sum to 1 per pixel)
   See: README_TRAINING_EXPLANATION.md (Section: The Objective)

Q: What's trained vs frozen?
A: Trained:  MLP network (adaptive weights)
   Frozen:   Physics, pulse, geometry (validated and correct)
   See: README_TRAINING_EXPLANATION.md (Table: What Gets Trained)
